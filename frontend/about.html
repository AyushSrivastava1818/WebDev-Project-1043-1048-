Heritage, Ethos & Leadership

Established 1948

Our Historic Foundation

St. Jude's Academy was founded in 1948 by a dedicated council of visionary educators and philanthropists who recognized the imperative of creating an institution rooted in high scholastic standards, ethical fortitude, and broad civic responsibility in the immediate aftermath of independence.

What began with forty students in a modest stone manor in Old Civil Lines has expanded into a sprawling 22-acre academic center recognized nationally as a premier institution affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).

School Motto: Veritas et Virtus

"Truth in Scholarship, Virtue in Action" – A guiding beacon reminding every St. Jude scholar that intellect without moral compass is incomplete.

Our Foundations

The Four Pillars of St. Jude's

Every academic program, co-curricular endeavor, and house championship is designed to reinforce our enduring four institutional pillars.

Pillar I

Scholastic Rigor

We cultivate critical thinking, evidence-based reasoning, and deep mastery of subjects rather than rote memorization, preparing scholars for premier universities worldwide.

Pillar II

Moral Integrity

Character building lies at the heart of our pastoral care. Honesty, respect for diverse viewpoints, and accountability guide every interaction on campus.

Pillar III

Civic Responsibility

Education serves society. Through structured community outreach, environmental action, and village literacy programs, scholars actively contribute to their nation.

Pillar IV

Holistic Resilience

Physical vitality through competitive athletics, artistic expression through music and fine arts, and mental endurance prepare students for life's tests.

Chronicles of Excellence

Milestones in Our Journey

1948 – Inception & Charter

Foundation

St. Jude's Academy formally established with 40 scholars and 6 founding educators under the presidency of Justice K.M. Banerjee.

1968 – CBSE Affiliation & Science Laboratories

Expansion

Secured permanent affiliation with CBSE; inaugurated the dedicated Sir C.V. Raman Science Wing and Olympic swimming pavilion.

1998 – Golden Jubilee & Central Library

50 Years

Celebrated fifty years of academic distinction with the commissioning of the 30,000-volume Tagore Memorial Library and Archives.

2024–Present – Digital Transformation & Innovation Labs

Modern Era

Deployed Atal Tinkering Lab, solar campus micro-grid, smart digital classrooms, and our institutional student information portal.
