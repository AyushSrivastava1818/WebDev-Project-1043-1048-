Senior Secondary Specialized Streams

Students in Senior Secondary may select between our three accredited faculties, each overseen by veteran subject masters with access to state-of-the-art facilities.

Medical & Non-Medical Tracks

Empirical Inquiry, Computational Science & Advanced Mathematics

The Faculty of Science prepares scholars for careers in Engineering, Medicine, Pure Sciences, and Technological Research. Theory is closely coupled with empirical laboratory experimentation, Olympiad training, and independent capstone research papers.

Core Subject Combinations:

  • Track A (PCM): English Core, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science / Physical Education
  • Track B (PCB): English Core, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics / Psychology / Biotechnology
  • Electives: Informatics Practices, Artificial Intelligence, Economics, Fine Arts

Evaluation Architecture

Assessment Scheme & Examinations

Continuous Internal Assessment (20%)

Comprising periodic classroom quizzes, hands-on lab journals, research portfolios, speaking/listening assessments (ASL), and project presentations.

Mid-Session Examinations (30%)

Formal mid-year descriptive examinations conducted under standardized testing conditions in October to evaluate cumulative conceptual retention.

Annual / CBSE Board Exams (50%)

Summative year-end board examinations evaluating complete syllabi, accompanied by external viva-voce and laboratory assessments.
