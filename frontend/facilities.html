Campus Architecture

Campus & Facilities

Spread across 22 verdant acres in Civil Lines, our campus bridges historic architectural elegance with contemporary research infrastructure.

Scholastic Resource

Tagore Memorial Central Library

Housing over 30,000 catalogued volumes, international research journals, an audio-visual archive, and quiet reading alcoves overlooking the campus heritage gardens.

  • JSTOR & EBSCO Digital Research Database Terminals
  • Rare Book Archive dating back to 1912
  • Automated RFID book circulation system
Empirical Research

Advanced Science Complex

Four independently certified laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Biotechnology, fully compliant with national laboratory safety protocols.

  • Individual workstation apparatus for 40 scholars simultaneously
  • Digital spectrometers and compound microscopes
  • Fume extraction hoods and chemical hazard neutralization
Athletic Mastery

Sir Arthur Sports Pavilion

A comprehensive athletic complex featuring standard playing fields, outdoor tracks, and indoor arenas coached by certified NIS trainers.

  • 400-meter all-weather synthetic running track
  • Natural turf football pitch & cricket pavilion
  • Semi-Olympic 25m heated indoor swimming facility
Technology & Design

Atal Tinkering & Robotics Hub

An innovation makerspace dedicated to prototyping, electronics, 3D printing, and software development supported by NITI Aayog guidelines.

  • Microcontroller prototyping benches (Arduino & Raspberry Pi)
  • Dual-extruder rapid 3D printing stations
  • High-speed fiber connectivity & Linux computing clusters
Performing Arts

Heritage Assembly Auditorium

An 800-seat acoustic proscenium theater constructed in teak and stone, hosting daily morning assemblies, guest lectures, and drama productions.

  • Surround acoustic treatment & stage lighting rig
  • Steinway concert grand piano for recitals
  • Green rooms and set fabrication workshops
Health & Dining

Infirmary & Dining Commons

Complete student wellbeing services featuring a 10-bed staffed medical ward and a hygienic dining commons serving nutritious, dietitian-curated meals.

  • Two full-time registered medical officers on campus
  • Dedicated tie-up with Fortis Hospital for emergency care
  • FSSAI-certified kitchen adhering to pure vegetarian standards
