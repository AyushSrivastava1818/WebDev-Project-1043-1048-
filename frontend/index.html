A Tradition of Rigor, Character, and Civic Responsibility

"Welcome to St. Jude's Academy. Since our establishment in the dawn of a newly independent nation in 1948, our mandate has remained steadfast: to instill intellectual curiosity alongside unyielding integrity. We do not merely prepare students for standardized assessments; we prepare them to navigate a multifaceted global landscape with empathy, reason, and purpose."

"Whether inside our state-of-the-art computational physics laboratories, across our athletic fields, or upon the stage of our heritage assembly hall, every scholar is challenged to discover their distinct potential."

Scholastic Framework

Comprehensive Academic Divisions

Our structured pedagogy aligns strictly with the Central Board of Secondary Education curriculum, enriched by research projects and interdisciplinary inquiry.

Foundational & Preparatory

Primary Wing (Grades I–V)

Emphasis on foundational literacy, mathematical reasoning, environmental observation, and expressive arts in an encouraging exploratory environment.

  • Experiential Mathematics & Science Play
  • Bilingual Language Arts (English & Hindi)
  • Art, Choral Music, and Fundamental Athletics
View Primary Curriculum
Middle School

Middle Years (Grades VI–VIII)

Transition to rigorous subject-specific disciplines, analytical research papers, scientific laboratory work, and civic leadership responsibilities.

  • Separate Physics, Chemistry & Biology Labs
  • Introduction to Coding & Robotics Systems
  • Third Language Options: Sanskrit / French / German
View Middle School Scheme
Board & Senior Secondary

Senior Secondary (Grades IX–XII)

Rigorous preparation for All India Secondary (AISSE) and Senior School Certificate Examinations (AISSCE) across three distinguished faculties.

  • Science: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, CS
  • Commerce: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics
  • Humanities: History, Pol Science, Psychology, Sociology
